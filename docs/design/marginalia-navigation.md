# Marginalia navigation

Spool uses margins as navigation and evidence surfaces. The margins are not decoration.

## Left margin

The left margin is the article index. It is recessed during reading, exposes a persistent `Articles` tab, expands on hover or focus, and can be pinned open.

It contains:

- article list
- active article marker
- featured marker
- tags when present
- evidence indicators
- reading progress

Keyboard behaviour covers Tab entry, arrow movement, Home/End, Enter, and Escape.

## Mobile

Hover does not exist on mobile. The article index moves into a drawer opened by an `Articles` button. The drawer scrolls internally and closes with Escape, a close button, or the backdrop.

## Right margin

The right margin is the evidence surface. It shows compact platform observability and event-source evidence. Panels are bounded and scroll internally.

## Visual direction

The metaphor is manuscript marginalia, not fantasy UI. Use thin rules, readable type, paper and ink colours, and restrained accents. Avoid heavy parchment textures, decorative clutter, glow effects, or navigation that feels like an app bar.
